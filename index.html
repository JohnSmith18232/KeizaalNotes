⚔️ Getting Started

📜

Keizaal Online is a serious roleplay project. The economy, professions, and social systems are designed for RP interaction, not solo grinding.

Your First Login

  • An onboarding menu will appear, explaining the rules, lore, and how to begin. Read it carefully.

  • On your first character you receive a pickaxe, an axe, and 50 septims to get you started.

  • By default your name is hidden from everyone. Press X (default key) to introduce yourself via the context menu before others can know who you are.

  • Open your Skills menu (K) to pick a profession and track your Exhaustion Timer.

  • The compass is disabled by default. You can re-enable it in the launcher settings.

You Have Two Characters

Each account supports two characters. If you want to try a different profession or playstyle, use your second slot rather than burning your reset. You also have one profession reset per character (available at the Guardian Stones near Riverwood).

Carry Weight

Your default carry weight is 200. Backpacks increase this. Keep an eye on what you pick up — over-encumbrance is very real.

🛠️ Professions

⚠️

Workbenches are locked to their profession. You cannot mine unless you are a Miner, forge unless you are a Blacksmith, cook unless you are a Cook, etc. Choose wisely — you only get one reset per character.

⛏️

Miner

Mines ore veins. Ore depletes and replenishes over time. Pickaxes can break on lower-tier ores.

🪓

Woodcutter

Chops firewood. Higher levels yield more firewood per chop.

🔨

Blacksmith

Forges weapons and armor. Access to forges and workbenches. Craft higher-tier items for XP multipliers (up to 3×).

🌾

Farmer

Only farmers can gather crops (wheat, cabbage, etc.). Whitelisted farming system.

🍖

Cook

Uses cooking pots and ovens. Exhausted cooks cannot craft.

🧵

Tailor

Uses the Loom to craft clothes. Master Tailors can perform face sculpts (costs 400 septims). Exhausted tailors cannot craft.

🏹

Hunter

Only hunters can loot animals (not creatures). Must set mastery as Hunter first.

⚔️

Warrior

The strongest PvP/PvE profession. +25 Stamina at Advanced, Expert, and Master (+75 total). Not designed for solo play — meant for guards, legions, bandits, mercenaries.

Exhaustion

Every XP gain exhausts your character. While exhausted you earn no XP (e.g., a Hunter won't gain XP killing NPCs). Your Exhaustion Timer is visible in the Skills menu (K). Crafting professions also cannot craft while exhausted.

XP Multipliers

Crafting professions earn up to 3× XP for crafting higher-tier items. Always aim for the best tier you can manage.

Resetting Your Profession

Visit the Guardian Stones near Riverwood. You get one reset per character — it resets both your profession and your XP. Use it wisely.

💰 Economy & Trade

💡

Because workbenches are profession-locked, trade between players is essential. A Blacksmith needs ore from Miners; a Cook needs crops from Farmers. Seek out other professions early.

🏠 Housing & Lockpicking

Getting a House

  • Find the Jarl of the hold you want to live in. Only the Jarl of that hold can grant you a house.

  • Stewards (appointed by Jarls) can also grant/revoke houses and chest access on their behalf.

  • Once granted, you are the Owner. You can add Guests via the Property Management Menu.

Doors & Locks

🔑

Every locked house and chest uses a master lock. Owners (and Guests) must manually unlock their door to enter and lock it again when they leave. Use /lockhouse to lock from inside.

Lockpicking is available for those who want to attempt entry. Exploits have been patched — don't bother trying.

Chests

All chest interactions are logged. The "Take All" bug has been fixed — items will no longer disappear. Owners can manage guest chest access through the Property Management Menu.

🗡️ Combat & Gear Loss

⚠️

Death has real consequences. You will drop items when killed by a player or NPC. Do not carry anything irreplaceable into dangerous situations.

Gear Loss on Death

  • Up to 2 items may drop from your inventory (can be 0 or 1 as well).
  • Lower probability of ingredients dropping; higher probability of equipment dropping.
  • Up to 10% of your septims are lootable.
  • Your coin pouch visually reflects how much gold you carry — bandits may notice.

Safe Zone

Windhelm's Pit does not cause any gear loss if you die inside it.

Restoration Spells

Restoration spells have been fixed — they heal you properly and ignore armor. Carry potions and healing spells when adventuring.

Dungeons

NPCs spawn only when you are close to them (optimised for performance). Chests inside dungeons give a random number of items and random gold.

🎭 Social & Roleplay

Identity

Everyone's name is hidden by default. Press X to open the context menu and introduce yourself. You must introduce yourself before others can know your character's name — and vice versa.

Disguises

Use /disguise to hide your identity visually. Vampires can use /vamp to toggle their vampire appearance to prevent metagaming — if you see a vampire with facial features visible, you may act on it in-character.

Voice Chat Modes

🔇 Whisper

Very short range. Use for private conversations.

🗣️ Local (Default)

Normal proximity range for general RP.

📢 Yell

Extended range. Use sparingly — it carries far.

Voice chat also includes lip sync (mouths may occasionally stay open — speaking again fixes it). RMS normalisation means all players sound roughly the same volume.

Governance (Jarls, Guards, Stewards)

  • Jarls control their hold: they can add Guards, grant/revoke houses in their hold, and appoint Stewards.
  • Guards can cuff and uncuff players.
  • Stewards can manage house and chest access on behalf of the Jarl.

✨ New Player Tips

💡

Pick a crafting or gathering profession first. Warrior is powerful but is meant for group RP (guards, bandits, mercenaries) — solo warriors have no economy role.

💡

Don't over-carry gold. Your coin pouch size is visible to other players. Keep valuables in your locked chest at home.

💡

Introduce yourself early. Nobody can see your name until you do. Starting interactions with "you are a stranger to me" is fully supported mechanically.

💡

Find your Jarl quickly if you want a house. Only the Jarl of a hold can grant you property in that hold.

⚠️

Save your profession reset. You get exactly one per character. Try your chosen profession for a while before deciding to reset.

📍

Guardian Stones are near Riverwood — that is where you go to reset your profession if needed.

🍺 Drinks (Alchemy)

📍

The only open alchemy table is in Windhelm. All three drinks below require Alchemy 15.

⚠️

All drinks slow Stamina regeneration by 30% for 30 seconds as a side effect — plan accordingly in combat.

Weight 0.5  ·  Value 5
